## Research Interests
I am interested in probability theory and its applications to physics and biology. My research focuses on stochastic PDEs, interacting particle systems, stochastic quantizations, stochastic modeling, Dirichlet forms.

## Work in Progress
1. **Spatial modeling for virus infection with multiple stages**, with Louis Fan and John Yin. *(In preparation)*
2. **Universality of stochastic FKPP equation**, with Louis Fan and Adrián González Casanova. *(In preparation)*
